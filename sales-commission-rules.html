Commission Terms

Operational requirements from the January 2026 policy.
  1. Achievement is calculated only from invoiced transactions.
  2. Achievement uses the amount before tax and net of returns and uncollectible receivables.
  3. Commission is processed in the fifth month after the transaction period and paid with month-end payroll.
  4. Regular commission requires both the personal minimum target and minimum store transaction target.
  5. Special Product commission requires the minimum value and minimum qualifying-category targets.
  6. Commission for resigned employees is held until their customer receivables are settled.
